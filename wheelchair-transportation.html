What this service covers

Wheelchair transportation is designed for riders who need accessible entry, securement support, and dependable scheduling for medical travel.

  • Hospital and clinic appointments
  • Dialysis and recurring therapy trips
  • Outpatient procedures and post-discharge travel
  • Family and caregiver coordination

Frequently asked questions

Can family or facility staff coordinate the trip?

Yes. Caregivers, family members, and authorized facility staff can request transportation and receive status updates when needed.

Can I schedule recurring wheelchair rides?

Yes. Recurring scheduling is available for dialysis, therapy, and ongoing medical appointments when the ride pattern is known in advance.

What is the best number to call?

Use the main public line at (888) 639-5766 for new ride requests and general coordination.
