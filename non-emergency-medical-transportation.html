Non-emergency medical transportation

Non-emergency medical transportation across Maryland, Washington, DC, and Northern Virginia

Nexus Medical Transit provides planned non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) for passengers who need more than a curbside ride. Our drivers provide door-through-door support — help from inside the pickup location, secure boarding, and a accompanied arrival at check-in — and our team coordinates trip details with caregivers and facilities so pickup, appointment, and return stay aligned. Book a single ride or set up recurring transportation for ongoing treatment.

Nexus provides planned, non-emergency transportation. If this is an emergency, call 911.

Choose the right ride

  • Ambulatory — walking passengers who need reliable, assisted rides to appointments.
  • Wheelchair transportation — accessible vehicles and securement for passengers who ride in their own chair.
  • Stretcher transportation — reclined transport for passengers who must remain lying down.
  • Bariatric transportation — higher-capacity vehicles and equipment, planned around the passenger.
  • Ambulance transportation (BLS/ALS) — EMT-staffed interfacility transfers and discharge transport.
  • Dialysis & recurring rides — standing schedules for treatment several times a week.
  • Hospital discharge — release-day rides coordinated with the care team.
  • Medical shuttle — scheduled shuttle support for facilities and programs.

Frequently asked questions

What is non-emergency medical transportation?

NEMT is planned transportation to healthcare — appointments, dialysis, therapy, discharge home, and facility transfers — for passengers who don't need emergency care but do need an accessible vehicle and assistance.

How far in advance should I book?

Book as early as you can; planned trips let us match the right vehicle and crew. Call (888) 639-5766 and we'll confirm what we can schedule.

Can a caregiver ride along or coordinate the trip?

Yes. Caregivers and authorized facility staff can help set up trip details and receive status updates.

What areas do you serve?

Maryland, Washington, DC, and Northern Virginia — see our Maryland, DC, and Northern Virginia pages for detail.
