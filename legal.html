Terms of Use

These Terms govern access to Nexus Medical Transit websites, booking tools, portals, communications, and transportation-coordination services. By using the services, you agree to these Terms.

In plain language: Provide accurate trip and mobility information, use the service safely and lawfully, and contact us promptly when plans change. Nexus coordinates transportation; availability and arrival times can be affected by traffic, weather, clinical discharge timing, and other circumstances.

1. Who may use the services

You must be legally capable of entering an agreement or use the services through a parent, guardian, caregiver, facility, health plan, broker, or other authorized representative. A person booking for someone else represents that they are authorized to provide the rider's information and request transportation.

2. Transportation services

Nexus coordinates scheduled transportation that may include ambulatory, wheelchair, stretcher, bariatric, shuttle, recurring-treatment, and other approved service levels. The service level provided depends on availability, the information supplied, payer authorization, applicable requirements, and operational or clinical review.

Except when a separately identified and appropriately licensed service is dispatched, Nexus transportation is not emergency response, medical diagnosis, treatment, or continuous clinical monitoring. Never use an online booking tool for an emergency.

3. Booking responsibilities

  • Provide accurate rider, pickup, destination, appointment, contact, mobility, equipment, assistance, and payer information.
  • Tell Nexus about stairs, accessibility barriers, weight or equipment requirements, infection-control precautions, behavioral or safety concerns, and any escort requirement relevant to safe transportation.
  • Ensure the rider is ready at the confirmed pickup time and that an authorized receiving person is available when required.
  • Update or cancel requests promptly. Cancellation, wait-time, no-show, and payer rules may apply.

4. Quotes, payment and benefits

Price estimates are based on information available when quoted and may change if mileage, wait time, tolls, service level, required personnel, equipment, or trip conditions change. Coverage or authorization by a health plan, government program, broker, facility, or other payer is not guaranteed. The responsible party remains responsible for charges not covered or authorized, to the extent permitted by law and contract.

5. Accounts and authorized portals

Keep credentials confidential, use only the access assigned to you, and notify Nexus of suspected unauthorized access. Facility, driver, dispatcher, billing, and administrative portals may only be used for authorized business purposes. Users may not scrape data, probe security, disrupt service, impersonate another person, or access records without a legitimate need.

6. Safety and rider conduct

Riders and companions must follow reasonable safety directions, use required restraints, and avoid threatening, discriminatory, unlawful, or dangerous conduct. Service may be delayed, modified, or refused when reasonably necessary to protect a rider, worker, provider, or the public, subject to disability-access and other applicable laws.

7. Service animals, mobility devices and companions

Nexus supports lawful access for service animals and riders with disabilities. Riders should provide advance information about mobility devices, dimensions, securement needs, stairs, transfers, escorts, or other assistance so an appropriate vehicle and crew can be considered. Contact Customer Service for an accommodation or alternative format.

8. Service availability

Requested or estimated pickup and arrival times are not guarantees. Nexus is not responsible for delays or interruptions outside its reasonable control, including traffic, severe weather, road closures, facility delays, vehicle incidents, network failures, emergencies, labor interruptions, or government actions.

9. Third-party services

Some trips, payments, maps, communications, or benefits may involve independent transportation providers or technology and payment vendors. Their services may be governed by additional terms. Nexus remains responsible for its own obligations but does not control every third party's independent acts.

10. Disclaimers and limits

To the extent permitted by law, digital services are provided on an “as available” basis. Nothing in these Terms limits rights or remedies that cannot legally be waived. Any limitation of liability, indemnification, arbitration, class-action waiver, or governing-law provision applicable to a particular contracted service will be stated in the controlling rider, facility, payer, provider, or other written agreement.

11. Changes and termination

Nexus may update these Terms to reflect service, legal, or operational changes. The date above identifies the latest version. Material changes will be communicated as required by law. Access may be suspended or terminated for misuse, fraud, safety threats, nonpayment, or legal or contractual requirements.

Consumer Health Data Notice

This supplemental notice applies to consumer health data that may be protected by state privacy law even when it is not PHI under HIPAA.

Important distinction: A pickup at a clinic, pharmacy, dialysis center, behavioral-health location, or other care destination may reveal or support an inference about a person's health. Nexus treats this information as sensitive.

Health data we may collect

  • Transportation needs, mobility information, accessibility requirements and requested level of service.
  • Pickup and destination information that identifies or may suggest a healthcare service.
  • Appointment timing, recurring treatment schedules, discharge status, relevant precautions, and authorized escort needs.
  • Eligibility, benefit, payer, authorization and healthcare-facility information.
  • Information another authorized person provides when arranging a trip.
  • Inferences reasonably necessary to select or coordinate an appropriate transportation service.

Why we collect and use it

We collect and use consumer health data only as reasonably necessary to provide or maintain a requested transportation service, communicate about it, support safety and accessibility, obtain authorization or payment, investigate incidents, comply with law, and carry out other purposes with consent.

Who may receive it

Consumer health data may be shared with the rider and authorized representatives, requesting facilities or providers, health plans and transportation brokers, assigned transportation personnel, and vendors that support necessary service functions. Nexus does not sell consumer health data and does not use it for targeted advertising.

Your choices

Subject to applicable law and exceptions, you may ask whether Nexus processes your consumer health data, request access or correction, request deletion, withdraw consent for consent-based processing, or appeal a denied request. Some information may need to be retained to complete a trip, document a service, process a claim, protect safety, or satisfy legal obligations.

HIPAA & PHI Practices

HIPAA applies only to particular information and relationships. Nexus handles PHI according to the role it performs in the applicable service arrangement.

This section is not automatically a HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices. When Nexus acts as a business associate, the healthcare covered entity's Notice of Privacy Practices generally describes the individual's HIPAA rights. A separate formal notice may apply to any Nexus operation determined to be a covered entity.

When Nexus acts as a business associate

Nexus may create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI on behalf of a healthcare provider, health plan, clearinghouse, broker, or another business associate. In that role, Nexus uses and discloses PHI as permitted by the applicable Business Associate Agreement, underlying services agreement, and law.

Our PHI commitments

  • Use or disclose PHI only as permitted by contract or law and apply the minimum-necessary standard where required.
  • Maintain safeguards appropriate to electronic and other PHI.
  • Report suspected impermissible uses, disclosures, security incidents, and breaches as required.
  • Require applicable subcontractors handling PHI to accept appropriate privacy and security obligations.
  • Assist the covered entity with legally required access, amendment, restriction, and accounting requests when applicable.
  • Return, destroy, or continue protecting PHI after termination as required by the controlling agreement and law.

Requests involving PHI

If your health plan, provider, facility, or broker arranged the transportation, it may be the best first contact for a HIPAA access, amendment, restriction, or accounting request. Nexus will route or support requests as required. Verification of identity and authority may be required before information is released.

Business Associate Agreements

Healthcare organizations seeking a BAA or information about Nexus privacy and security practices should contact contact@nexusmt.com. A BAA is used when required by HIPAA; signing a BAA does not by itself make unrelated information or activity subject to HIPAA.

Communications Terms

Nexus may communicate by telephone, email, portal notification, or text message about transportation and account activity.

Service communications

Communications may include booking confirmations, driver or vehicle details, pickup status, delays, cancellations, recurring-trip reminders, account security, payment, support, and safety information. Message frequency varies. Carrier message and data rates may apply.

Text-message choices

By providing a mobile number through a disclosed Nexus booking or enrollment flow, you agree to receive transactional booking and ride updates. Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to end texts and HELP for assistance. Stopping messages may limit real-time trip updates but does not cancel a scheduled trip. Call Customer Service to change communication preferences or cancel transportation. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Nexus transportation messages do not contain alcohol, tobacco, cannabis, gambling, adult, or other age-restricted promotional content.

Privacy of communications

Ordinary email and SMS may not be fully secure. Nexus limits sensitive content in messages where practical. Do not send unnecessary medical details by unsecured email or text. Use an authorized secure portal or call Nexus when sensitive information must be discussed.
