About Nexus

Mission-first medical transportation centered on access, equity, and clear coordination.

Nexus Medical Transit helps riders, caregivers, and facilities coordinate planned medical transportation with accessible service options, communication, and dependable scheduling across Maryland, Washington, DC, and Northern Virginia.

Our mission

Access drives equity. We focus on transportation coordination that respects rider needs while helping caregivers and facilities stay aligned on timing, mobility, and destination details.

  • Compassionate support for riders and families
  • Clear communication across stakeholders
  • Structured workflows for recurring and one-time trips
  • Accessibility-aware transportation coordination

How we coordinate rides

We support planned non-emergency transportation through a single workflow that can be used by riders, authorized caregivers, and facilities.

  • Wheelchair transportation
  • Stretcher transportation
  • Dialysis and recurring treatment travel
  • Hospital discharge transportation
