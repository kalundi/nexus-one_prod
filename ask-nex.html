Nex's answer

Source pages

    AI-assisted responses can be incomplete or outdated. Verify important trip and care arrangements with Nexus directly.

    How Nex answers

    Nex searches Nexus's approved website knowledge and uses that information to answer questions. Some responses may be phrased using an AI model; Nex also works using prewritten, retrieved answers when AI service is unavailable.

    What Nex can and can't do

    • Nex can explain general services, accessible transportation, coverage, and how to request a ride.
    • Nex cannot confirm a live ride booking or guarantee availability from this page.
    • Fare estimates require trip details and use the separate booking and estimate process.
    • Nex does not diagnose conditions, recommend a patient's clinical level of transport, or provide emergency response.

    Privacy and accuracy

    Do not enter patient names, diagnoses, medical records, payment details, or other sensitive personal information here. Questions are sent to the Nexus assistant service to generate a response. The public assistant uses a small amount of conversation history kept in this page's memory to understand follow-up questions. Reloading or closing the page clears it; this page does not save chat history to browser storage.

    Website updates are gathered for review; they are not automatically approved as answers. The Nexus team controls the approved knowledge catalog.

    Medical emergency? Call 911. For confirmed service information or booking assistance call (888) 639-5766.
