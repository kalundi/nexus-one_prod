Bariatric ride service

Bariatric transportation planned around the passenger — capacity, comfort, and dignity

Standard vehicles don't fit every passenger, and a ride that starts with "will it fit?" starts wrong. Nexus plans bariatric transportation in advance: the right vehicle, the right equipment — higher-capacity wheelchair securement or stretcher — and a crew briefed on the pickup location, so there are no surprises at the door. Tell us the passenger's mobility equipment and any access details (steps, doorways, elevator) when you book, and we match the trip to it.

Nexus provides planned, non-emergency transportation. If this is an emergency, call 911.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer bariatric transportation near me?

We serve Maryland, Washington, DC, and Northern Virginia. Call (888) 639-5766 with your pickup and destination and we'll confirm coverage for your trip.

Can bariatric trips be recurring?

Yes — standing schedules are available for ongoing treatment when the pattern is known.

What if I'm not sure which vehicle we need?

Call us. We'll ask about the passenger and equipment and assign the right vehicle — that's a planning conversation, not a guess at pickup.
