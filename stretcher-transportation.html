Frequently asked questions

Can facilities schedule stretcher transportation?

Yes. Hospitals, rehabilitation centers, skilled nursing facilities, and discharge planners can coordinate trips when rider details and timing are ready.

Is this intended for emergencies?

No. For emergencies, call 911. This page is for planned, non-emergency medical transportation.

What number should patients or facilities use first?

Call the main public line at (888) 639-5766 to coordinate the trip details.
