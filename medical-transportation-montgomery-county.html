Medical transportation in Montgomery County — from Clarksburg to Bethesda

Nexus Medical Transit is based in Clarksburg, in the heart of Montgomery County, and coordinates non-emergency medical transportation across the county: wheelchair, stretcher, bariatric, dialysis, and hospital discharge rides with door-through-door support. Local trips are our shortest routes — and recurring treatment rides to the county's dialysis centers, hospitals, and specialty practices are the trips we run most.

Nexus provides planned, non-emergency transportation. If this is an emergency, call 911.

Frequently asked questions

Are you based in Montgomery County?

Yes — Nexus operates from Clarksburg, MD, and serves all of Maryland, Washington, DC, and Northern Virginia.

Can I set up recurring dialysis rides in Montgomery County?

Yes. Standing schedules can be arranged for ongoing treatment — call (888) 639-5766.

Do you serve addresses outside the county line?

Yes — trips into DC, Northern Virginia, and the rest of Maryland are part of our regular coverage.
