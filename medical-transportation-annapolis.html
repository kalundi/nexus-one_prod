Medical transportation in Annapolis and Anne Arundel County

Nexus Medical Transit coordinates planned medical transportation across Annapolis and Anne Arundel County for passengers, families, and facilities: wheelchair and stretcher rides, recurring dialysis transportation, and hospital discharge trips planned around the release time — not around our schedule. Facilities in the Annapolis area can coordinate transfers through one booking workflow, and families can track the ride with LiveCare from pickup to arrival.

Nexus provides planned, non-emergency transportation. If this is an emergency, call 911.

Frequently asked questions

Can a facility in Annapolis book discharge transportation with Nexus?

Yes. Discharge teams can coordinate individual rides or recurring transfers — start with booking or call (888) 639-5766.

Do you run trips from Annapolis to DC or Baltimore hospitals?

Yes — longer regional trips are part of our Maryland/DC/Northern Virginia coverage.

Can family members track the ride?

Yes, authorized caregivers can follow trip status through LiveCare.
